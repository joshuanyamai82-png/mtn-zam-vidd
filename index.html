FAQ

Questions Zambians ask us

Who can apply for a MTN Momo Loans loan?

You need to be 18 years or older, a Zambian resident, and have a valid National Registration Card (NRC), an active mobile money number, and a regular source of income.

How much can I borrow in Kwacha?

Between K500 and K50,000 depending on your profile. First-time borrowers usually start smaller — as you repay on time, your limit grows automatically.

How fast will I get the money?

Most applications get a decision within about 10 minutes. Once approved and verified, funds are sent to your mobile money wallet or bank account, usually within 24 hours.

What does it cost?

We charge a flat monthly interest rate plus a once-off arrangement fee, both shown clearly before you accept. Nothing is deducted without your knowledge.

Can I repay early?

Yes. You can settle your loan at any time and you'll only pay interest for the period you actually used the money — no early settlement penalty.

What happens if I miss a payment?

A late fee applies and we'll contact you to arrange a new date. We'd rather work out a plan with you than report you — but repeated missed payments do affect your ability to borrow again.

Ready to get your Kwacha?

Apply in under three minutes. You'll need your phone number, and your mobile money wallet.
